Overview

Overview

Submersible mixers are used in wastewater treatment systems for liquid agitation, sludge agitation, sedimentation prevention, homogenisation and flow circulation — common duties in biological tanks, equalisation basins and sludge holding tanks.

The range covers different power ratings, speeds, impeller diameters and mounting arrangements, selected according to the tank and site conditions. Special installation arrangements can be designed on request.

Features

Features

01

Wide model coverage

The PJ and PJM series cover a range of power ratings, pole numbers, impeller diameters and speeds to match tank size and mixing requirements.

02

Prevents sludge settling

Continuous flow circulation prevents suspended solids from settling and keeps concentration uniform inside the tank.

03

Submersible design

The complete unit operates submerged — no separate machine room is required and running noise is low.

04

Flexible installation

Several mounting arrangements are available to suit tank geometry, water depth and maintenance access, with custom designs available on request.

Installation

Installation

The mounting arrangement is chosen to suit tank geometry, water depth and maintenance access; custom designs are available.

Arrangement 1: Guide rail lifting

The mixer is raised and lowered along a guide rail, allowing removal for maintenance without emptying the tank. Suitable for deep tanks and regular servicing.

Arrangement 2: Floor mounted base

Fixed to a base on the tank floor. Once the angle is set, operation is stable — suitable for shallower tanks with a fixed mixing direction.

Arrangement 3: Wall bracket

Mounted on a wall bracket with adjustable horizontal and tilt angles, making the flow pattern inside the tank easier to control.

Arrangement 4: Custom design

For unusual tank geometries, existing structures or retrofit projects, a dedicated mounting arrangement can be designed to suit the site.
