曝氣系統

Aeration System

Fine bubble diffusers matched with centrifugal blowers, balancing oxygen transfer efficiency against energy use.

Aeration is one of the largest electricity consumers in a treatment plant. Raising the oxygen transfer efficiency of the diffusers lowers the air volume required, which in turn reduces blower power. We supply both diffusers and blowers, and can evaluate the configuration against tank size, water depth and oxygen demand.

污水攪拌

Wastewater Mixing

Submersible mixers for sedimentation prevention, homogenisation and in-tank flow circulation.

Without adequate mixing, anoxic tanks, equalisation basins and sludge holding tanks suffer from solids settling, uneven concentration and reduced treatment efficiency. Submersible mixers are selected by power, impeller diameter and mounting arrangement to maintain the flow pattern.
